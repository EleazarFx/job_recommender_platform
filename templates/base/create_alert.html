About Job Alerts
What are Job Alerts?

Job alerts automatically notify you when new jobs match your search criteria.

Notification Frequencies:
  • Instant: Get notified immediately (best for competitive positions)
  • Daily: Receive a digest once per day
  • Weekly: Get a weekly summary (best for passive job searching)
Tips:
  • You can pause or delete alerts anytime
  • Create different alerts for different job types
  • Use location to focus on specific areas
